Gavin McGarry Prototype 6

Programming the controls for tilting the maze went well.
The Unity physics engine feels very janky at times and I had trouble getting it to cooperate.
The ball would often clip through the ground or take a second to actually start rolling and I am still not happy with the acceleration of the ball.
Next time I would tweak the ball's acceleration and make the execution of rotating the maze more physics engine friendly.
